Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026.

This policy explains how VersoUni handles information when you use the app.

Information you provide

VersoUni may store your student profile, saved universities and programs, application records, notes, attachments, visa-preparation progress, and appointment details. Sign in with Apple may provide an account identifier and, when you permit it, your name and email address.

How information is stored

Your profile, application drafts, saved items, visa records, and attachments are stored locally on your device. Your Sign in with Apple identity is stored securely in the device Keychain. Deleting the app or using the in-app deletion controls may remove this local information.

CloudKit and university catalogue

VersoUni uses Apple CloudKit to retrieve public university and program catalogue updates. The public catalogue does not contain your profile, application drafts, or visa-preparation records.

Subscriptions

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. VersoUni receives transaction and entitlement information needed to determine whether Premium access is active. We do not receive your full payment-card details.

Notifications

If you grant permission, VersoUni uses notifications for reminders you choose to create, including application deadlines and visa appointments. You can change notification permission in device Settings.

Third-party services and websites

The app opens official university, consulate, visa, and other third-party websites. Information you submit on those services is governed by their own privacy policies and is not controlled by VersoUni.

Sharing and selling

VersoUni does not sell your personal information. Information may be handled by Apple services used for authentication, subscriptions, CloudKit, and notifications, subject to Apple’s policies.

Your choices

You can edit or delete locally stored information from the app. You can sign out of Apple, disable notifications, and manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple Account settings.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to versouni.support@gmail.com.
